# Resource Hub — Therapist Feedback Plan

The Resource Hub must be shaped by the therapists who will use it. Before
(and during) content build-out, gather answers to these questions — in a team
meeting, a shared doc, or one-on-ones. Capture answers per therapist.

## Questions

1. **Folders** — What folders would you use most? What's your mental model
   for finding a resource in a hurry (by diagnosis? therapy area? age)?
2. **Diagnoses first** — Which diagnosis categories should be populated
   first? (Our caseload skew: ASD? ADHD? GDD?)
3. **Therapy areas first** — Which therapy areas should be first?
   (Handwriting? Sensory? Emotional regulation? ADLs?)
4. **Recreated worksheets** — What worksheets do you constantly recreate
   from scratch? (These are the highest-value uploads.)
5. **Product links** — What products do you regularly recommend to families?
   Include the low-cost Kmart/Big W alternatives you actually suggest.
6. **Session ideas** — What session ideas would most help a new therapist
   joining Opal?
7. **Parent handouts** — What parent/carer handouts are missing or always
   needed?
8. **School resources** — What do teachers/EAs keep asking for?
9. **PD & research** — What PD/research resources should be included?
   What do you wish you'd been given in your first month?
10. **Client suggestions UX** — When the portal suggests resources for one
    of your clients, how should that appear? Where would it be useful
    (before a session? during planning?) and where would it be annoying?
11. **Never suggest automatically** — What should NEVER be suggested
    automatically (e.g. feeding/safety-critical resources, behaviour
    protocols, anything requiring assessment first)?

## How feedback feeds the build

| Answers to | Shape |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Folder + seeded tag structure (R1) |
| 4, 6–8 | First upload wave + templates (post-R1 content) |
| 5 | Product-link repository priorities (R2) |
| 9 | PD section (R4) |
| 10 | Client-suggestion UI (R5/R6) |
| 11 | Suggestion exclusion rules — hard-coded before R5 ships |

## Ground rules to share with the team

- Everything uploaded goes through review before other therapists see it
  (draft → review → approved).
- Cite sources; note copyright. If we can't share it legally, we link to it
  instead of hosting it.
- Product links need safety notes (choking, supervision, age suitability).
- Suggestions will always say: *"Therapist review required before use."*
