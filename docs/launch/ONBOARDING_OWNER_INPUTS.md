# Onboarding: what the practice still has to supply

*For the owner walkthrough. Prepared 22 Oct 2026 from the documents seeded in the portal (`seeds/content/onboarding_document_versions.json`) and the onboarding audit. Nothing here has been written or answered by the developer — every item is the practice's own decision or wording.*

## How the portal treats these gaps now

- **A document that still contains "[TO CONFIRM …]", a "PLACEHOLDER - …" file, a "Dev QA content" stub, or an empty file can never reach a starter.** The Release button, the starter's Acknowledge button, the contract/position-description upload and the (secondary) paper pack all refuse it, and the refusal names the document.
- So **no onboarding can be released** until every policy the package asks the starter to acknowledge has been finished and published (Onboarding → Documents → the policy → new version → Publish), and the package re-published.
- Each record's **Employment contract** and **Position description** are attached on the record itself (Stage 2 → "The starter's contract and position description"). The contract can be generated from the contract template and the agreed terms.
