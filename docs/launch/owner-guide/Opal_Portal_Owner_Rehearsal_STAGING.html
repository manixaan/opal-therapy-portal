Before you start

Gather these first ✨

Having everything beside you makes setup quick. Nothing here is compulsory on the day — every setup step can be skipped and finished later — but the more you add now, the smarter the portal is from day one.

About you

  • The email address the practice set up for you as Owner
  • Your mobile number
  • A new password in mind (8+ characters, with a capital, a small letter and a number)
  • Your home address — where your working day usually starts

Your credentials (scans or photos)

  • Ahpra registration
  • Professional indemnity insurance
  • Working with Children Check
  • NDIS Worker Screening Check
  • Police check, first aid and CPR certificates
  • OT qualification and driver's licence

For the calendar move

  • Your Outlook calendar open in another window or on a second screen
  • A notepad (paper or digital) to jot down anything that feels clunky — that's gold for us
How staging differs from production. Staging is the test portal: it already has an Owner and some test accounts, it uses the practice's real Splose clients (read-only), invitation emails are not sent (you copy the link instead), and it restarts for a minute whenever a new version is pushed. Enter real appointments to rehearse, but don't rely on anything kept here.
Please don't connect Outlook yet. In this first stage the portal is a "read-only rehearsal": everything you enter appears in the portal, but nothing is written to Outlook or Splose. We'll switch those on together once the calendar is in and checked.

Part 1 · Getting in

Open the portal and create your account

1

Open the portal

In Chrome, Edge or Safari, go to:

https://opal-portal-staging.azurewebsites.net

This is the test portal. The live one (production) is a different address — don't mix them up.

2

Invite yourself as an Owner

Staging already has an Owner, so Create account won't make you one. Instead:

  • Sign in with the existing staging Owner account.
  • Go to Settings → Users & Roles → Invite team member.
  • Enter the email you'll rehearse with, role Owner, tick Treating therapist, 7 days.
  • Click Send invite. Email isn't on in staging, so you'll see "Invite created — email NOT sent" and a Copy link button.
  • Sign out (or open a private window) and paste the link.
Production does this differently: the real Owner uses Create account with the pre-approved email and becomes Owner straight away. The invitation route you're using here is what staff will see later — so you're rehearsing both.
3

Sign in

You'll land on the sign-in page. Enter your email and new password and click Sign in.

Forgot it already? Use Forgot password? under the sign-in button — a reset link arrives by email and works for one hour.

Part 5 · Afterwards

What happens next

1

Sync Splose — together

Open Sync Splose. For each appointment the portal shows whether it's already in Splose: Link it, or Ignore it permanently. Nothing new is created in Splose at this stage.

2

Then Outlook

We clear your Outlook calendar, connect it, and send your portal calendar across once. From then on the portal is where you make changes.

3

Then writing on

Once you trust it, the portal starts updating Splose and Outlook as you book, move and cancel.

4

Then your team

Invite staff from Settings → Users & Roles. New employees go through onboarding in the portal; you press Activate when they're ready.
