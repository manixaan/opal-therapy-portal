# Opal Therapy Portal — Therapist Quick-Start Guide

Welcome! This portal is the practice's internal scheduling hub. Five
minutes here will save you a lot of questions. (Pilot note: you're the
first therapist using this — some things are deliberately switched off,
and your feedback shapes what comes next.)

## 1. Getting in

- **Access**: use Chrome or Edge on a laptop. The owner will send you your
  personal registration link (there's no public sign-up). Phones aren't
  supported yet.
- **Log in** with your email and the password you set at registration. If
  you ever forget it, tell the owner — automatic reset emails may not be
  switched on yet.

## 2. First-time setup (10 minutes)

- The **onboarding wizard** walks you through your details, work locations
  and preferences. Finish all steps — the review step at the end matters.
- **My Profile → Account setup status** is your truth panel: it shows
  exactly what's done and what's pending (profile ✓, Splose link, Outlook,
  travel base) with next steps. If something says *pending — practice
  owner*, that's the owner's job, not yours.
- **Confirm your home/travel base** under Profile → Work location — travel
  time estimates depend on it.

## 3. Connect Outlook (the important one)

- Go to **Settings → Integrations → Connect Outlook** and sign in with
  your **practice Microsoft account** (not a personal one).
- If the calendar shows **“Outlook not connected”** — that's honest, not
  broken: click Connect. After connecting, the first sync takes a minute
  or two ("first sync running…").
- Connected? The pill shows **your own mailbox address**. If it ever shows
  something odd, tell the owner. **Disconnect** (same screen) stops sync
  for your account only and never changes anything inside Outlook.

## 4. Your calendar

- The calendar is a **mirror of your Outlook** (plus practice data from
  Splose). Outlook is the source of truth — the portal never writes to
  your Outlook or to Splose during the pilot.
- The header pill shows **real** sync ages (click it to refresh). Data
  also refreshes itself every couple of minutes.
- Weekends hidden? That's the **Show weekends** toggle in Settings →
  Calendar.
- “Outlook-only” is an owner/admin comparison view — you won't see it.

## 5. Splose data

- You can see **your own appointments** (matched to your Splose
  practitioner profile). You will NOT see other therapists' caseloads,
  the whole client directory, or any billing — that's by design.
- If you see **“not linked to a Splose practitioner yet”**, the owner
  hasn't completed your link — nudge them; nothing is wrong on your side.
- Booking clients happens **in Splose directly** during the pilot; the
  portal's booking wizard is view-only for therapists.

## 6. Leave, documents, resources

- **Leave**: My Profile → Leave → request. The owner approves and will
  tell you directly (no automatic notification yet, and approved leave
  doesn't block Splose bookings yet — coordinate with the owner).
- **PD documents**: upload certificates etc. under My Profile (5 MB max);
  you can download them back any time.
- **Resource Hub**: browse approved practice resources by folder, search
  or tags; ⭐ favourites. Start with *Induction / new therapist resources*.
  Adding content goes through the owner for now.

## 7. What's NOT available yet (so nothing surprises you)

Booking/writing to Splose or Outlook from the portal · automatic
leave-notifications and calendar blocking · phone/tablet layout · flight
tracking (tab is disabled) · schedule PDF export · adding your own Hub
resources. Anything marked **“Preview only — not saved”** means exactly
that.

## 8. Privacy expectations

Client information you see is real practice data — treat it per the
practice's privacy policy and NDIS practice standards. Don't screenshot
client data, don't use the portal on shared computers without logging out,
and don't note clinical content anywhere except the practice's clinical
system (Splose).

## 9. Pilot ground rules + help

- If something looks wrong (calendar mismatch, odd names, errors): don't
  work around it silently — **tell the owner** (screenshot + what you
  clicked). You can't break anything: all writes to external systems are
  disabled.
- Help: practice owner (Antony) first, for anything.
